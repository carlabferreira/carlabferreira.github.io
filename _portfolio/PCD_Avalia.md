The project aims to develop an application prototype for people with disabilities, their friends and family, business owners, and general users interested in checking establishment reviews. To achieve this, personas, scenarios, task modeling, and cognitive walkthroughs were created during the Human-Computer Interaction (HCI) course at UFMG.

### Objective and Key Features:
To develop an application prototype that assesses the accessibility of public and private establishments, aiming to provide information to people with disabilities.

**Features:**
- User registration and login
- Search for establishments by name and accessibility filters
- Rate establishments with stars and comments
- Add a new establishment
