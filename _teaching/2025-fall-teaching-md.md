During the first semester of 2025, I served as a Voluntary Undergraduate Teaching Assistant (TA) for the Discrete Mathematics course under the supervision of Prof. Dr. Antonio Loureiro. 

With a 10-hour weekly workload, my key responsibilities included:

- **Mentoring**: Clarifying doubts and guiding students through the online forum and one-on-one in-person office hours.
- **Assessment**: Grading the final practical projects.
- **Support**: Assisting in exam proctoring and covering for the professor during lectures when necessary.
